## ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS

### 1.1 "Confidential Information"

Means any and all information, in whatever form or medium, disclosed by either Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party"), including but not limited to:

**Technical Information:**
- Source code, algorithms, and software architecture
- Zoe DHF AI system specifications
- Protocol Ironclad encryption methodology
- Soul Codex data structures
- Phoenix Protocol preservation technology
- Matter Bridge IoT integration
- Database schemas and APIs

**Business Information:**
- Business plans and strategies
- Financial information and projections
- Customer and user data
- Pricing and cost information
- Marketing strategies
- Partnership and vendor relationships

**Proprietary Information:**
- Trade secrets
- Inventions and patents (pending or issued)
- Design documents
- Product roadmaps
- Research and development data

### 1.2 "Authorized Representatives"

Means employees, directors, officers, advisors, contractors, and agents of the Receiving Party who:
- Have a need to know the Confidential Information for the Purpose
- Are bound by confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement

## ARTICLE 2: OBLIGATIONS

### 2.1 Protection of Confidential Information

The Receiving Party shall:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│           CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS                    │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ ✓ Protect with same care as own confidential info       │
│ ✓ Use at least reasonable care (not less than)          │
│ ✓ Use only for the stated Purpose                       │
│ ✓ Limit disclosure to Authorized Representatives        │
│ ✓ Not reverse engineer, decompile, or disassemble       │
│ ✓ Not copy except as reasonably necessary               │
│ ✓ Return or destroy upon request                        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2.2 No Unauthorized Disclosure

The Receiving Party shall NOT:
- Disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent
- Use Confidential Information for any purpose other than the Purpose
- Remove or alter any confidentiality markings or legends
- Disclose the existence or terms of this Agreement without consent

### 2.3 Employee and Contractor Obligations

Before disclosing Confidential Information to any Authorized Representative, the Receiving Party shall:
- Inform them of the confidential nature
- Ensure they are bound by written confidentiality obligations
- Be responsible for any breach by such representatives

## ARTICLE 3: EXCLUSIONS

### 3.1 Exceptions to Confidentiality

Confidential Information does NOT include information that:

| Exception | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Public Knowledge** | Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of Receiving Party |
| **Prior Knowledge** | Was known to Receiving Party prior to disclosure (with documentation) |
| **Independent Development** | Was independently developed without use of Confidential Information |
| **Third Party Source** | Was lawfully obtained from a third party without restriction |
| **Written Release** | Is released in writing by the Disclosing Party |

### 3.2 Burden of Proof

The Receiving Party bears the burden of proving any exception applies.

---

## ARTICLE 4: COMPELLED DISCLOSURE

### 4.1 Legal Requirements

If the Receiving Party is compelled by law, regulation, or legal process to disclose Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall:

1. **Promptly notify** the Disclosing Party (to the extent legally permitted)
2. **Reasonably cooperate** with the Disclosing Party's efforts to seek protective measures
3. **Limit disclosure** to only what is legally required
4. **Request confidential treatment** from the compelling authority

### 4.2 Survival

Such compelled disclosure shall not constitute a breach of this Agreement, provided the Receiving Party complies with Section 4.1.

## ARTICLE 5: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

### 5.1 No License Granted

Nothing in this Agreement grants the Receiving Party any rights to:
- Patents, copyrights, trademarks, or trade secrets
- Licenses (express or implied) to any intellectual property
- Ownership of any Confidential Information

### 5.2 Retained Rights

All Confidential Information remains the exclusive property of the Disclosing Party.

### 5.3 Residual Knowledge

The Receiving Party's personnel may retain in unaided memory general knowledge, ideas, concepts, know-how, and techniques contained in the Confidential Information, provided they do not infringe any intellectual property rights.

## ARTICLE 6: RETURN OF INFORMATION

### 6.1 Upon Request or Termination

Upon written request or termination of this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall:

```
RETURN/DESTRUCTION PROTOCOL
============================

Within 15 business days:
├── Return all physical materials containing Confidential Information
├── Delete all electronic copies from all systems
├── Delete all copies from cloud storage and backups
├── Provide written certification of destruction
└── Except: Retain copies required by law/regulation (marked confidential)
```

### 6.2 Certification

The Receiving Party shall provide written certification of compliance upon request.

## ARTICLE 7: TERM AND TERMINATION

### 7.1 Term

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for **[TERM: e.g., "2 years"]** unless earlier terminated.

### 7.2 Termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement:
- With 30 days written notice to the other Party
- Immediately upon material breach by the other Party

### 7.3 Survival

The confidentiality obligations shall survive termination for:

| Information Type | Survival Period |

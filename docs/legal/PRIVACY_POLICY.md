3. Notify you (unless legally prohibited)
4. Fight overbroad requests

## 7. DATA RETENTION

### 7.1 Retention Periods

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
|-----------|-----------------|--------|
| Account Data | Until deletion | Service operation |
| Soul Codex | Until deletion | Your AI companion |
| Conversations | Until deletion | Context and memory |
| Location Data | 90 days (then anonymized) | Feature operation |
| Usage Analytics | 2 years (anonymized) | Service improvement |
| Security Logs | 7 years | Legal compliance |
| Phoenix Data | Until activation or deletion | Legacy preservation |

### 7.2 Deletion Process

When you delete your account:
1. **Immediate:** Account deactivated, data inaccessible
2. **30 days:** Soft delete (recoverable if you change your mind)
3. **60 days:** Hard delete from primary systems
4. **90 days:** Purged from backups
5. **Cryptographic shredding** of encrypted data

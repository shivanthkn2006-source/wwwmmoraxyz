## ARTICLE 2: TERM AND TERMINATION

### 2.1 Term

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of **[24/36] months** unless earlier terminated.

### 2.2 Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement:
- With 30 days written notice for any reason
- Immediately upon material breach by the other party
- Immediately upon mutual written agreement

### 2.3 Effect of Termination

Upon termination:
- All vested equity shall be retained by the Advisor
- Unvested equity shall be forfeited
- Confidentiality obligations shall survive
- Advisor shall return all Company materials

### 5.1 Confidential Information

The Advisor acknowledges that they will receive confidential information, including:
- Business plans and strategies
- Financial information
- Technical specifications
- User data and analytics
- Zoe AI architecture and Soul Codex protocols
- Partnership and investor discussions

### 5.2 Obligations

The Advisor agrees to:
- Maintain strict confidentiality of all Confidential Information
- Use Confidential Information only for providing Services
- Not disclose to any third party without prior written consent
- Return or destroy all materials upon termination

### 5.3 Exceptions

Confidential Information does not include information that:
- Is or becomes publicly available (through no fault of Advisor)
- Was known to Advisor prior to disclosure
- Is independently developed by Advisor

### 5.4 Duration

Confidentiality obligations survive termination indefinitely for trade secrets and for 3 years for other confidential information.

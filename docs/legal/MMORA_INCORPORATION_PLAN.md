FOUNDERS AGREEMENT

BETWEEN: Saraswathi (Moksh) ("Founder")
AND: MMORA Holdings Pte Ltd ("Company")

DATE: [Execution Date]

1. CONTROL PROVISIONS

1.1 Super Voting Rights
The Founder holds Class B Ordinary Shares with voting 
rights of ten (10) votes per share, compared to one (1) 
vote per share for Class A Ordinary Shares.

1.2 Board Control
The Founder shall have the right to appoint and remove 
a majority of the Board of Directors at all times, 
regardless of shareholding percentage.

1.3 Protective Provisions
The following actions require Founder approval:
- Amendment of Constitution
- Issuance of new share classes
- Sale or merger of the Company
- Removal of Founder as CEO
- Changes to Founder compensation

2. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

2.1 Assignment
All IP created by the Founder prior to and during 
employment is assigned to the Company.

2.2 Perpetual License
The Company grants to its Indian subsidiary a perpetual, 
royalty-free license to use all IP for operational purposes.

3. VESTING

3.1 Founder Vesting
The Founder's shares are 100% vested immediately upon 
execution of this Agreement.

3.2 Future Co-Founder Vesting
Any future co-founders or key employees shall be subject 
to a 4-year vesting schedule with a 1-year cliff.

4. COMPENSATION

4.1 Initial Salary
The Founder shall receive an initial salary of 
[Amount] per month, subject to Board approval.

4.2 Equity Refresh
The Founder shall be entitled to annual equity grants 
as determined by the Board.

5. TERM AND TERMINATION

5.1 This Agreement shall remain in effect until terminated 
by mutual written consent.

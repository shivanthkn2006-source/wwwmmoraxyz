# MMORA EMPLOYEE STOCK OWNERSHIP PLAN (ESOP)

**Document Classification: CONFIDENTIAL**  
**Version: 1.0**  
**Effective Date: January 2026**

---

## PREAMBLE

This Employee Stock Ownership Plan ("ESOP" or "Plan") is established by **MMORA Holdings Pte. Ltd.** (the "Company") to attract, retain, and motivate exceptional talent by providing them with an ownership stake in the Company's success.

---

## ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS

### 1.1 Key Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Award** | The grant of Options or RSUs under this Plan |
| **Board** | The Board of Directors of the Company |
| **Cause** | Gross misconduct, breach of fiduciary duty, or violation of employment terms |
| **Exercise Price** | The price per Share at which an Option may be exercised |
| **Fair Market Value (FMV)** | As determined by the Board or independent valuation |
| **Option** | The right to purchase Shares at the Exercise Price |
| **Participant** | An eligible person who receives an Award |
| **RSU** | Restricted Stock Unit - a promise to deliver Shares upon vesting |
| **Shares** | Ordinary shares of the Company |
| **Vesting Cliff** | Initial period before any Awards vest |
| **Vesting Period** | Total duration over which Awards vest |

## ARTICLE 3: ELIGIBILITY

### 3.1 Eligible Participants
The following persons are eligible to receive Awards:

1. **Employees** - Full-time and part-time employees of the Company or Subsidiaries
2. **Directors** - Non-executive directors approved by the Board
3. **Advisors** - Strategic advisors with signed advisor agreements
4. **Consultants** - Key consultants with ongoing engagements

### 3.2 Eligibility Criteria

| C-Level Executive | 0 months | 1.0% - 3.0% |
| VP/Director | 0 months | 0.5% - 1.5% |

## ARTICLE 4: VESTING SCHEDULE

### 4.1 Standard Vesting

```
STANDARD 4-YEAR VESTING WITH 1-YEAR CLIFF
═════════════════════════════════════════

Year 0-1 (Cliff Period)
│
├─ Month 1-11: 0% vested
│
└─ Month 12: 25% vests (Cliff)

Year 1-4 (Monthly Vesting)
│
├─ Month 13-48: Additional 2.083% per month
│
└─ Month 48: 100% fully vested

Timeline:
├────────────────┼────────────────┼────────────────┼────────────────┤
0               12              24              36              48
│    CLIFF       │     50%        │     75%        │    100%        │
│   (0% → 25%)   │               │               │   FULLY VESTED │
```

### 4.2 Accelerated Vesting Events

| Event | Acceleration |
|-------|--------------|
| **Acquisition (Single Trigger)** | 50% of unvested Awards |
| **Acquisition + Termination (Double Trigger)** | 100% of unvested Awards |
| **IPO** | Board discretion |
| **Death of Participant** | 100% immediate vesting |
| **Permanent Disability** | 100% immediate vesting |

### 4.3 Founder Special Terms

- **100% Immediate Vesting** on all founder shares
- No cliff period applicable
- Super Voting Rights (10:1) preserved

## ARTICLE 5: STOCK OPTIONS

### 5.1 Types of Options

| Type | Tax Treatment | Eligibility |
|------|---------------|-------------|
| **ISO** (Incentive Stock Options) | Favorable capital gains | US employees only |
| **NSO** (Non-Qualified Stock Options) | Ordinary income at exercise | All participants |
| **EMI** (Enterprise Management Incentive) | Favorable UK treatment | UK employees only |

### 5.2 Exercise Price
- Shall not be less than 100% of FMV on grant date
- ISO for 10%+ shareholders: minimum 110% of FMV

### 5.3 Exercise Period

| Termination Reason | Exercise Window |
|-------------------|-----------------|
| Voluntary Resignation | 90 days post-termination |
| Termination without Cause | 90 days post-termination |
| Termination for Cause | Immediate forfeiture |
| Death or Disability | 12 months |
| Retirement | 12 months |

### 5.4 Option Exercise Process

```
1. Participant submits Exercise Notice
         │
         ▼
2. Company verifies vested shares available
         │
         ▼
3. Participant pays Exercise Price
   ├── Cash exercise
   ├── Cashless exercise (if public)
   └── Net exercise (share withholding)
         │
         ▼
4. Company issues Shares to Participant
         │
         ▼
5. Tax withholding applied if applicable
```

---

## ARTICLE 6: RESTRICTED STOCK UNITS (RSUs)

### 6.1 RSU Terms
- No exercise price (settled at $0)
- Settled in Shares upon vesting
- Subject to same vesting schedule as Options
- Dividend equivalents may be accrued

### 6.2 Settlement
- RSUs automatically settle upon vesting
- Shares delivered within 30 days of vesting
- Tax withholding at settlement

---

## ARTICLE 7: TRANSFERABILITY

### 7.1 General Restriction
Awards are non-transferable except:
- By will or laws of descent
- To family trusts (with Board approval)
- To the Company (buyback)

### 7.2 Right of First Refusal (ROFR)
The Company retains ROFR on all Share transfers until IPO.

---

## ARTICLE 8: TERMINATION SCENARIOS

### 8.1 Voluntary Resignation

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────┐
│         VOLUNTARY RESIGNATION          │
├────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Vested Options:   Retain for 90 days   │
│ Unvested Awards:  Forfeit immediately  │
│ RSUs (vested):    Settled at departure │
│ RSUs (unvested):  Forfeit immediately  │
└────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 8.2 Termination for Cause
- ALL Awards (vested and unvested) immediately forfeit
- Company may claw back exercised shares within 12 months

### 8.3 Termination without Cause
- Same as voluntary resignation
- Board may, at discretion, accelerate partial vesting

---

## ARTICLE 9: CHANGE OF CONTROL

### 9.1 Definitions
**Change of Control** means:
- Acquisition of 50%+ of voting shares
- Sale of substantially all assets
- Merger where Company is not surviving entity
- Change in majority of Board without Board approval

### 9.2 Treatment of Awards

| Scenario | Treatment |
|----------|-----------|
| Awards assumed by acquirer | Continue with equivalent terms |
| Awards NOT assumed | 100% acceleration |
| Participant terminated within 12 months of CoC | 100% acceleration (Double Trigger) |

---

## ARTICLE 10: TAX CONSIDERATIONS

### 10.1 Tax Withholding
Company shall withhold applicable taxes at:
- Option exercise (NSO)
- RSU vesting
- Sale of shares

### 10.2 Section 409A Compliance (US)
All Awards designed to comply with or be exempt from IRC Section 409A.

### 10.3 International Tax
Participants in India, Singapore, and other jurisdictions subject to local tax laws.

---

## ARTICLE 11: CLAWBACK PROVISIONS

### 11.1 Clawback Events
Company may claw back Awards or proceeds if Participant:
- Engaged in fraud or misconduct
- Breached confidentiality or non-compete
- Caused material restatement of financials

### 11.2 Clawback Period
Extends 24 months from exercise/settlement date.

---

## ARTICLE 12: PLAN AMENDMENT AND TERMINATION

### 12.1 Board Authority
The Board may amend, suspend, or terminate the Plan at any time.

### 12.2 Participant Consent
Amendments that materially adversely affect existing Awards require Participant consent.

### 12.3 Plan Duration
The Plan shall remain in effect until terminated by the Board or exhaustion of the ESOP pool.

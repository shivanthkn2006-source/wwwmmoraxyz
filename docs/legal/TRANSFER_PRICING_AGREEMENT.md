## ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS

**"Arm's Length Principle"** means the principle that transactions between related parties should be conducted as if the parties were independent entities dealing with each other at arm's length.

**"Comparable Uncontrolled Transaction"** means a transaction between unrelated parties that is comparable to the controlled transaction.

**"Cost Plus Method"** means the transfer pricing method that uses costs incurred by the service provider, plus an appropriate markup.

**"IRAS"** means the Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore.

**"ITAA"** means the Indian Income Tax Act, 1961.

**"Related Party"** means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with another entity.

**"Services"** means the services described in Schedule A.

**"Transfer Pricing Documentation"** means the documentation required under applicable regulations.

**1.2 "Board"** means the Board of Directors of the Company.

**1.6 "MMORA Platform"** means the entirety of the technology stack including Zoe DHF, Soul Codex, Project Phoenix, Selfie City, and all related systems.

**1.8 "Subsidiary"** means MMORA Technologies Pvt Ltd (India) and any future subsidiaries.

## ARTICLE 2: SHARE CAPITAL AND OWNERSHIP

### 2.1 Initial Share Issuance

Upon incorporation, the Company shall issue to the Founder:

| Share Class | Number of Shares | Voting Rights | Ownership |
|-------------|------------------|---------------|-----------|
| Class B (Founder) | 10,000,000 | 10:1 | 100% |

### 2.2 Share Class Rights

**Class B Shares (Founder Shares):**
- Ten (10) votes per share on all matters
- Full dividend rights equal to Class A
- Anti-dilution protection on future issuances
- Automatic conversion to Class A upon transfer (except to Founder affiliates)
- Right to appoint majority of Board

**Class A Shares (Investor Shares):**
- One (1) vote per share on all matters
- Full dividend rights
- Standard liquidation preference as negotiated

### 2.3 Anti-Dilution Protection

In the event of any future share issuance, the Founder shall have the right to participate pro-rata to maintain ownership percentage. Additionally:

- No share issuance shall dilute Founder below 51% voting control without Founder consent
- Any "down round" shall trigger full ratchet anti-dilution for Class B Shares

### 2.4 No Forced Conversion

Class B Shares shall not be subject to mandatory conversion under any circumstance, including:
- IPO or direct listing
- Change of control
- Passage of time
- Any contractual provision

## ARTICLE 3: GOVERNANCE AND CONTROL

### 3.1 Board Composition

**Initial Board:**
- Founder (Chairman and CEO)
- One Independent Director (appointed by Founder)
- One Investor Director (upon funding, nominated by lead investor)

**Board Control:**
- Founder shall have the right to appoint and remove a majority of Directors at all times
- Quorum requires Founder presence
- Chairman (Founder) has casting vote in case of tie

### 3.2 Officer Positions

The Founder shall hold the following positions:
- Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
- Chairman of the Board
- Chief Technology Officer (CTO) - until separately appointed

**Removal Protection:**
The Founder cannot be removed from any officer position except by:
- Voluntary resignation
- Supermajority (75%) Board vote AND
- 66% shareholder vote (by shares, not voting power)

### 3.3 Protective Matters (Founder Consent Required)

The following actions require affirmative consent of the Founder:

**Corporate Structure:**
- [ ] Amendment of Constitution or this Agreement
- [ ] Creation of new share classes
- [ ] Issuance of shares, options, or convertible instruments
- [ ] Any merger, acquisition, or sale of the Company
- [ ] Dissolution or liquidation
- [ ] Change of jurisdiction

**Financial:**
- [ ] Annual budget approval
- [ ] Expenditure exceeding $100,000 (single transaction)
- [ ] Debt financing exceeding $500,000
- [ ] Related party transactions
- [ ] Dividend declarations

**Operational:**
- [ ] Hiring or termination of C-level executives
- [ ] Change of business strategy or core product
- [ ] Entry into new geographic markets
- [ ] Licensing of core IP to third parties
- [ ] Settlement of claims exceeding $50,000

**Founder-Specific:**
- [ ] Changes to Founder compensation or benefits
- [ ] Termination of Founder employment
- [ ] Restrictions on Founder activities

**Immediate Full Vesting:**
The Founder's Class B Shares are 100% vested immediately upon execution of this Agreement. There is no vesting schedule, cliff, or acceleration events applicable to Founder shares.

**Rationale:**
The Founder has already contributed years of work, the complete technology stack, and the vision for MMORA. The IP being assigned has substantial value that exceeds any future contribution timeline.

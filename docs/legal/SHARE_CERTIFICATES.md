## NOTES

1. All share certificates must be signed by two directors or one director and the company secretary.

2. The company seal must be affixed to each certificate.

3. Lost certificates require a statutory declaration and indemnity before replacement.

4. Transfer of Class B shares requires Board approval and triggers potential automatic conversion.

5. ESOP shares are issued upon exercise of vested options.

2. **🔄 Multi-Step Reasoning**
   - Breaks down complex tasks into steps

3. **🔧 Tool Calling**
   - Uses 6 powerful tools autonomously

   - Suggests actions before you ask

5. **📊 Deep Insights**
   - Analyzes patterns across all your activity
   - Synthesizes data into actionable recommendations
   - Provides personalized intelligence

6. **🎯 Goal-Oriented**
   - Understands your objectives
   - Creates detailed action plans
   - Helps execute plans step-by-step

## ❓ FAQs

### Q: How is agentic AI different from regular Zoe?
**A**: Regular Zoe responds to commands. Agentic Zoe proactively thinks ahead, makes autonomous decisions, and uses tools to complete complex tasks.

### Q: Can I disable agentic features?
**A**: Yes, in Zoe Settings you can adjust autonomy levels or disable specific tools while keeping basic voice features.

### Q: How does Zoe decide which tools to use?
**A**: Gemini 2.5 Pro analyzes your request, understands the context, and autonomously selects the optimal tools to achieve your goal.

### Q: Is my data safe?
**A**: Absolutely. All tool execution is scoped to your account only, encrypted, and follows strict privacy policies.

### Q: Can Zoe make mistakes?
**A**: Zoe is highly intelligent but can occasionally misinterpret intent. You can always provide feedback to correct course.

### Q: How do I know what Zoe is doing?
**A**: Zoe always explains her reasoning, which tools she used, and why she made specific recommendations.

### Q: Does agentic AI cost more?
**A**: Agentic features are included in your platform access. They may use more AI credits due to complex processing.

## Timeline Control Commands

### Playback Controls
- "play timeline" → Starts automatic timeline scroll
- "pause timeline" / "stop timeline" → Pauses timeline animation
- "start timeline" → Resumes timeline playback

### Speed Controls
- "speed up" / "faster" → Increases timeline speed by 0.5x
- "slow down" / "slower" → Decreases timeline speed by 0.5x

**Speed Range:** 0.5x to 3.0x (default: 0.5x)

### Zoom Controls
- "zoom in" → Increases zoom level by 20%
- "zoom out" → Decreases zoom level by 20%

**Zoom Range:** 50% to 300%
